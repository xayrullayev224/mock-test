📖 Reading Passage
The Impact of Social Media on Education

Social media has become an integral part of modern life, especially for younger generations. Platforms such as Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube have transformed how people communicate, consume information, and even learn.

Proponents argue that social media can be a powerful educational tool. It enables students to access a vast range of learning materials, participate in online discussions, and collaborate with peers across the globe.

However, critics point out that social media can be a significant distraction. Studies have shown that excessive use is associated with lower academic performance, reduced attention spans, and increased anxiety.

Despite these concerns, many institutions are finding ways to integrate social media into their curricula. By setting clear guidelines and promoting digital literacy, schools can help students harness the benefits while minimizing drawbacks.
